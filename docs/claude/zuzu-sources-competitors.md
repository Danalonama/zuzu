# Zuzu — competitors & new source leads (2026-08-13 scan)

## Competitive positioning
No **direct** competitor — no other "where do I move today" calendar for the Israeli movement scene.
- **Paid workshop directories** (basalon.co.il, kolsadna.co.il, "כל ריטריט"): broad/commercial/kids/ballroom, mostly bookable/date-less, paid placement. NOT sources, NOT direct competitors — they're *monetization references* (sponsored listings, reviews, gift cards, "add your workshop").
- **Aggregators / super-sources** (bodyways/דרך גוף, ci-events.org, ecstaticdance.co.il, biodanza): dated movement activities, single-source/discipline — already used as Zuzu sources.
- **Moat:** curated, dated, cross-discipline community movement scene (contact/ecstatic/gaga/biodanza/jams/somatic) in one "today" view. Uncontested niche.
